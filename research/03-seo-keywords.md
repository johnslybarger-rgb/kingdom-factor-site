## On-page SEO priorities

- One clear H1 per page.
- Start each page with a direct answer to who it serves and what it offers.
- Use extractable paragraphs for AI answers.
- Add FAQ blocks for objection handling.
- Use schema candidates: `Organization`, `WebSite`, `WebPage`, `Person`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Book`, and `Event` for webinars when dates are real.
- Include `llms.txt`, schema endpoints, sitemap index, robots.txt, and schemamap in the final technical build.
- Avoid keyword stuffing. This audience needs trust and specificity.
