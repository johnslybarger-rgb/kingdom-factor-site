# Kingdom Factor Product Context

register: brand

## Audience

Kingdom Factor speaks to Christian CEOs, business owners, senior executives, founders, and emerging owners who are carrying decisions that affect people, money, culture, stewardship, family, calling, and faith. They are not looking for generic inspiration. They need a trusted advisory room, qualified counsel, practical business wisdom, and faith-integrated accountability.

## Primary customer tension

The owner or executive is responsible for decisions no one else can carry. They may have a team, spouse, board, pastor, or friends, but they often lack a confidential room of peers and advisors who understand both business pressure and biblical conviction.

## Core promise

Kingdom Factor helps Christian business leaders stop leading alone by matching them with peer advisory, executive coaching, marketplace ministry resources, and a practical pathway toward trusted counsel.

## Positioning principles

- Speak directly to owners and executives, not vaguely to leaders.
- Make the pain concrete: isolation, hard decisions, blind spots, growth pressure, stewardship, culture, accountability.
- Make the outcome concrete: better decisions, trusted counsel, peer accountability, faith-integrated business leadership, next-step clarity.
- Do not overclaim statistics, member counts, testimonials, or credentials without approval.
- Do not sound like a church event, networking club, Bible study, or generic coaching funnel.
- Competitors such as C12 and Convene lead with isolation, performance, impact, monthly peer advisory, coaching, and qualified Christian peers. Kingdom Factor should be more intimate, advisory, story-rich, and high-trust.

## Offers

- Peer Advisory Groups for Christian owners and senior leaders.
- Executive Coaching Intensive for established owners, executives, and senior leaders.
- Peer Community for emerging owners, founders, and solopreneurs.
- Leadership Circle and Marketplace Ministry resources as lower-friction entry points.
- Books, webinars, and Collective stories as authority assets.

## Conversion goal

Get the right person to request a fit conversation or explore the path that matches their season. CTAs should be visible above the fold on mobile and desktop.
